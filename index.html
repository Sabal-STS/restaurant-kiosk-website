The path of one order

Six steps, one system.

An order enters at the table, the counter or the kiosk, and the same system carries it to the kitchen, onto the bill and out to the tax office. Here is every step it takes.

01 · TAP

A guest chooses a dish on the kiosk, or a waiter taps it on the floor.

02 · ORDER

The order is validated and recorded against the table, room or takeaway.

03 · TICKET

A kitchen order ticket is created the moment the order is placed.

04 · KITCHEN

The printer agent on your kitchen machine sends it to your KOT printer.

05 · BILL

The invoice is built with the tax already worked out, and a PDF comes with it.

06 · TAX OFFICE

The billing module prepares the CBMS payload and sends it to the IRD.

That took you two seconds. It takes your kitchen none.

Billing

CBMS is not an add-on here. It is in the billing module.

Nepal wants restaurant invoices reported to the Inland Revenue Department through CBMS. STS builds the invoice, works out the tax, prepares the CBMS payload and sends it. The PDF is generated by the system. You are not keeping a second set of books.

Under it

Boring where it should be boring.

Every restaurant on the system gets its own separate database. Questions

Questions you are about to ask.

Does it make bills the tax office accepts?
The billing module talks to CBMS directly. It builds the invoice, records the tax, prepares the CBMS payload and handles the response. Invoice PDFs are generated by the system.
Can I run two restaurants on one system?
Yes. Each one gets its own separate database. Their menus, orders and bills never touch.
Will it work with my kitchen printer?
The KOT Printer Agent runs on your kitchen machine and talks to your printer locally, so printing does not depend on a round trip to a server far away.
How do guests pay at the kiosk?
Fonepay, eSewa, Khalti and cash.
Do I have to install anything?
There is a Windows app if you want one. The admin side also runs in a browser.
Is another restaurant's data anywhere near mine?
No. Each restaurant has its own separate database, and the separation is enforced by the database itself, not just by the software.
What does it cost?
It depends on how many terminals and which modules you want. Ask on the demo and you will get a straight number.
